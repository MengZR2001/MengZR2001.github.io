Zhaorui Meng

I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Informatics at Xiamen University, advised by Prof. Shihui Guo. I received my B.Sc. in Information and Computational Mathematics from Xiamen University in 2023 and pursued graduate studies in pure mathematics, focusing on algebraic graph theory, before entering the Ph.D. program in 2025. From October 2025 to May 2026, I also conducted remote research under the supervision of Prof. Yipeng Qin at Cardiff University.

My research lies at the intersection of human motion modeling, computer graphics, and embodied intelligence. Drawing on my mathematical background, I am interested in how the structure and physical properties of human motion can inform learning algorithms. My work has explored structure-aware regularization for sparse inertial motion capture and the role of dynamics in the learning difficulty of physics-based motion imitation, alongside collaborative work on efficient physical simulation and motion evaluation.

More broadly, I am interested in how embodied agents can learn from human behavior and anticipate the physical consequences of their actions. My current interests include vision-language-action (VLA) models and world models for embodied intelligence, particularly how motion priors and physical structure can support more generalizable action learning and planning.
